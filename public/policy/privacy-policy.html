1. Overview

Primal Male is a wellness and lifestyle app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have when using our app and related services.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data: email address and login/authentication data.
  • Onboarding and lifestyle inputs: questionnaire responses, goals, and preferences.
  • Mission and progress data: daily mission completion and progress metrics.
  • Subscription data: plan status and billing-related metadata.
  • AI coach inputs (optional): prompts and generated reports when AI features are used.
  • Technical data: service logs and diagnostic data needed to operate and secure the service.

3. How we use information

  • Provide core app functionality, authentication, and account management.
  • Deliver mission tracking, progress insights, and personalized wellness outputs.

4. Service providers and sharing

We use third-party processors to run the app:
