Request Account Deletion

Effective date: June 19, 2026

Company: Easy Sahal LLC
App: Primal Male

You can request deletion of your Primal Male account and associated personal data at any time. Follow the steps below.

1. How to request deletion

  1. Open the Primal Male app on your device.
  2. Go to Settings → Contact Support.
  3. Send a message with the subject Account deletion request.
  4. Include the email address linked to your account.

Alternatively, contact Easy Sahal LLC using the same email address you use to sign in to the app.

2. What we delete

  • Your account (email and authentication record)
  • Profile and onboarding data
  • Health, habit, mission, and progress tracking data
  • App preferences and settings

3. What we may keep

  • Payment and subscription records processed by Stripe, retained as required for tax, accounting, and fraud prevention
  • Encrypted backups containing your data for up to 90 days, after which they are removed

4. Before you delete

If you have an active subscription, cancel it in the app under Settings → subscription management, or via the Stripe customer portal, before requesting deletion.

5. Processing time

We process deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by email when your account and associated data have been removed from our active systems.

6. Contact

For account deletion questions, contact Easy Sahal LLC.
