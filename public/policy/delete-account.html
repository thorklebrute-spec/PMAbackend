Delete Your Account

Effective date: September 14, 2026

Company: Easy Sahal LLC
App: Primal Male

You can delete your Primal Male account and associated personal data at any time from the app. Deletion is immediate.

1. How to delete your account

  1. Open the Primal Male app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm twice. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

If you cannot open the app, contact Easy Sahal LLC from the email address you use to sign in and ask for account deletion.

2. What we delete

  • Your account (email and authentication record)
  • Profile and onboarding data
  • Health, habit, mission, and progress tracking data
  • App preferences and settings

3. What we may keep

  • Payment and subscription records processed by Stripe, retained as required for tax, accounting, and fraud prevention
  • Encrypted backups containing your data for up to 90 days, after which they are removed

4. Subscriptions

If you have an active subscription, deleting your account cancels it immediately so you are not billed again. You do not need to cancel separately first.

5. Processing time

In-app deletion removes your account and associated personal data from our active systems immediately. Email requests are processed within 30 days.

6. Contact

For account deletion questions, contact Easy Sahal LLC.
